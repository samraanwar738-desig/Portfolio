How it started

One home oven, one simple promise

Golden Crumb started in 2013 as a single home oven and a promise: no shortcuts, no preservatives, just honest baking. Today we're a full bakery team, but every loaf is still shaped by hand and every cake is still decorated one at a time.

We source local flour and dairy, ferment our doughs slowly for real flavour, and taste-test everything before it reaches your table.
